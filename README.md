# Vice Cover Maker

A browser app that turns your own photos and a caption into GTA-style multi-panel cover art with a Vice City sunset or neon grade, exported as PNG.

Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6 Cover Art" bounty, $250 in USDC.

## Features

- Multi-panel poster layout: one large hero panel plus two stacked panels with clean gaps and borders.
- Bring your own photos: load one photo or a different photo per panel, straight from your machine. Nothing is uploaded.
- Position every panel: drag a photo to reframe it, zoom with the slider or mouse wheel.
- Two colour grades: warm Vice City sunset and magenta/cyan neon, applied over the whole cover.
- Bold caption lettering across the cover in a heavy condensed stack, with an optional subtitle line.
- Export as PNG in the common post sizes: 1080 x 1080, 1080 x 1350, and 1920 x 1080.
- Runs fully offline from static files in `app/` — open `app/index.html` in a desktop browser, or preview locally with `node serve.ts`.
